### Glad you're here!

Hey! I'm **Roberto**, a **Tax Transformation Manager** at a Big4 firm, based in Monterrey, México 🇲🇽. I have **10+ years** in Finance & Tax Transformation working with Fortune 500 companies — Baker Hughes, ESAB, MAHLE and GE, among others — designing how technology, data and processes meet to solve real tax and finance problems.

Outside of work, I **learn by building**: I spin up **personal technical study projects** and **proofs of concept** to explore ideas — fiscal automation, data systems, AI agents — and understand first-hand how software comes together *end to end*, from the data model to the last UI component. I'm trilingual (**Español · English · Português**) and I believe the best technology is the kind that becomes **invisible and accessible** for the people who use it 🚀

- **🗄️ Data end to end** — I enjoy modeling data with **PostgreSQL**, **Drizzle ORM** and **Supabase**, orchestrating everything with **Node.js**, **Docker** and **Vercel**.

- **🧘 Contemplative side** — I'm deeply into **personal development**, **nervous system regulation**, **breathwork** and **meditation**, plus learning about **theology** and **philosophy**. As happy debugging code as sitting in silence.
- **🎌 Geek both ways** — Also an **anime & manga** fan (*The Apothecary Diaries*, *One Piece*) — good company for thinking about stories, systems and well-built worlds.
- **🌱 Currently learning** — Agent interoperability protocols, generative UI, and AI governance frameworks.
