### Glad you're here!

Hey! I'm **Roberto** — a **Tax Transformation Manager** at a Big4 firm, based in Monterrey, México 🇲🇽. I have **10+ years in Finance & Tax Transformation and technology**, across Fortune 500 companies and Big4 consulting.

It started with an intern who had no budget, automated 70% of a financial close, and earned a CEO Award — and the throughline never changed: turning fragmented, manual finance into scalable, system-enabled platforms across **R2R, P2P, O2C, consolidation and tax**, with automation, analytics, AI and process mining under **TOGAF, PRINCE2, SAFe, Lean and CRISP-DM**.

Outside of work, I **learn by building**: I spin up **personal technical study projects** and **proofs of concept** to explore ideas — fiscal automation, data systems, AI agents — and understand first-hand how software comes together *end to end*, from the data model to the last UI component. I like to say the best architecture you'll ever design is the internal one — so I apply the same rigor inward.

I'm trilingual (**Español · English · Português**) and I believe the best technology is the kind that becomes **invisible and accessible** for the people who use it 🚀

- **🔁 Record-to-Report & financial close** — automation and redesign of close, reporting and reconciliation processes.

- **🧾 Tax compliance, e-invoicing & e-mandates** — tax data governance, CFDI/SAT, and the global wave of e-invoicing / e-accounting mandates (LATAM + the OECD landscape worldwide).
- **⛏️ Process mining & analytics** — discovering real-vs-expected flows, root-cause analysis, decision-grade dashboards.
- **🏭 Smart industries / Industry 4.0** — process mining, RPA and predictive-maintenance use cases.
- **🏛️ Solution architecture & AI enablement** — ERP configuration, integration architecture, and bringing agents/automation into back-office workflows responsibly.
